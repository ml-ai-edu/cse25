# CSE 25: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

**Teaching materials for an early introduction to ML/AI**

CSE 25 is an undergraduate course developed at the University of California San Diego for students early in their computing education, including students pursuing an AI specialization. The course introduces core ideas in artificial intelligence through machine learning, without requiring college-level mathematics and with only introductory programming experience assumed.

Students explore supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and reinforcement learning through scaffolded activities, small datasets, programming exercises, and projects. The course also addresses how AI systems affect people and society.

This repository shares materials for instructors who want to develop or adapt a similar course. The materials can be adapted to suit different curricula and student populations.

## Course materials

- **[Lectures](Lectures/):** Lecture materials and examples.
- **[Discussions](Discussions/):** Discussion-section activities and supporting materials.
- **[Programming assignments](Programming%20Assignments/):** Hands-on assignments exploring AI/ML concepts.
- **[Final project](Final%20Project/):** Materials for the course project.

Materials may contain Jupyter notebooks, PDFs, and other files. Check individual activities for setup instructions, dependencies, and supporting datasets.

## Using and adapting the materials

Instructors are welcome to explore the course sequence, adapt activities, and select materials appropriate for their students. The course was designed as an *introduction* to AI/ML, not as a substitute for later, more mathematically rigorous courses.

Before using materials in a new setting, review the notebooks and assignments for local dependencies, course-specific instructions, and any third-party content.
